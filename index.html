The Estimate-to-Invoice Pipeline

A free Google Sheets + Apps Script tool that carries an accepted estimate into a job and into an invoice with no retyping. Approved change orders reach the bill, a pending change order blocks invoicing loudly, and the status board leads with the money question nothing free answers: which jobs are finished but were never billed, for how much, for how long. A to-the-cent reconciliation prints DO NOT TRUST THIS BOARD if the numbers stop tying out.

What it found on the sample business

  • 6 finished jobs that were never invoiced — $33,845.44, the oldest done 136 days ago
  • An earlier build that matched invoices by customer name saw only 5 of the 6, and called the oldest 62 days
  • 8 of 17 invoices underbilled by a combined $3,244.31 — deposits taken off before tax, and approved change orders that never reached the bill

How it was tested

The shipped engine was checked against an independent Python implementation on 240 generated businesses — 166,836 fields compared, zero disagreements — plus 37 functional tests and a settings sweep where every setting is proven to change the output on the shipping sample data. The episode shows the wrong turns too; they're part of the point.
